About Sanjivani Library & Study Center

Sanjivani Library & Study Center is the premier study destination in Sangrampur, providing students with a peaceful and conducive environment for learning. Our library is equipped with modern facilities, extensive book collections, and comfortable study areas.

We cater to students preparing for various competitive exams including JEE Main, NEET, UPSC Civil Services, CLAT, CAT, and many more. Our mission is to provide quality study infrastructure and support to help students achieve their academic goals.

Library Rules & Regulations

Please follow these rules to maintain a peaceful study environment

Holiday Rules

  • Library remains closed on all government holidays
  • Closed on alternate Sundays
  • Advance notice provided for special closures

Study Area Rules

  • Maintain complete silence in study areas
  • Sit only on your allotted seat
  • Mobile phones must be on silent/vibrate mode
  • Use headphones for online classes
  • Keep personal belongings secure
  • No wandering outside designated areas

Cafeteria Rules

  • Lunch area only for meals, not for discussion
  • Lunch timing: 12:00 PM - 3:00 PM
  • Keep lunch boxes in your bag
  • Dispose garbage in dustbins only
  • Switch off lights and fans when leaving

General Rules

  • Use flush after using bathroom
  • Close water taps properly
  • No smoking or drinking inside premises
  • Inform staff about any disturbance
  • No unauthorized visitors allowed
  • Violation may result in membership cancellation

"If anyone is unable to follow these rules, they must leave the library as their behavior disturbs other students."
